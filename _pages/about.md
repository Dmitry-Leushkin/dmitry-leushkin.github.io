I am an independent researcher and human systems architect based in Saint Petersburg, Russia. My work examines how language, meaning, biological inputs, and nutrition can shape regulation under sustained cognitive and practical demands.

Since 2008, I have developed the Turbo-Suslik system as a long-running case of structured, self-directed cognitive practice. That work has produced a substantial record of user correspondence, field observations, implementation difficulties, and reports of change over time. I use this material to develop careful descriptive models rather than clinical or therapeutic claims.

My current research includes language-based cognitive processing, semantic cueing, long-term observational analysis, functional nutrition, and applied mycology. I also build research-data systems that preserve source material, structure evidence, and make analytical work easier to inspect.

I publish technical reports and working papers through [HOF Research](https://hof-research.org/), the Human Optimization Framework. HOF is an independent research initiative, not a clinical or medical institution. Its publications distinguish established evidence, observation, exploratory models, and hypotheses.

Research interests
======

* Protocol-based cognitive processing and semantic regulation
* Longitudinal observation and research-data systems
* Functional nutrition for high cognitive load
* Applied mycology and fungal biomass cultivation
